CONFERENCES & SUMMITS ATTENDED

Learning beyond the syllabus.

I enjoy attending conferences and summits because they offer opportunities to learn beyond the syllabus and textbooks. They expose me to new ideas, useful resources, emerging technologies and perspectives that can inspire further research and project ideas.

DCIIS Summit

View More

Data Centre Infrastructure & Innovation Summit UAE (DCIIS UAE 2026) 📍 Dusit Thani Dubai, UAE 📅 29–30 June 2026, This is where I learned more about the rapid growth of the UAE and Middle East data-centre sector and the role of AI and digital technologies in driving this growth. A major focus of the discussions was also on building more sustainable and energy-efficient data-centre infrastructure. Hearing from professionals such as H.E. Eng. Yousif Ahmed Al Ali, Eng. Mariam Al Ali, Ali Al Madani from Khazna Data Centers, and representatives from Masdar City and Heriot-Watt University.

Elastic Day

View More

I attended Elastic Day Dubai at Kempinski Dubai, where I learned more about how AI is being used in areas such as search, cybersecurity and observability. The event focused on the shift from generative AI towards agentic AI and how organisations are beginning to apply these technologies to real-world operations. I found the discussions around AI, data transformation and digital resilience particularly interesting, especially seeing how organisations such as e& are using technology to improve their systems and services.

Manage Engine

View More

The ManageEngine UserConf 2026 held at The Ritz-Carlton Dubai, DIFC, where I got to explore more about how organisations are moving from experimenting with AI to using AI-driven and autonomous systems. The event covered topics including agentic AI, cybersecurity, identity and access management, threat detection and IT automation. I also had the opportunity to hear from industry professionals such as Rajesh Ganesan, CEO of ManageEngine, Heba Farahat, Offensive Security Tech Lead at Liquid C2 MENA, and Nirmal Kumar Manoharan, Vice President of Revenue Operations at ManageEngine.

Mawarid FINTECH Summit

View More

I attended the Mawarid FINTECH Summit, where I explored developments in financial technology, digital innovation and emerging technologies. The summit provided insights into how technology is transforming financial services and creating new opportunities for innovation.

NVIDIA Partner

NVIDIA’s work in AI connects closely with my interest in Computer Science.
